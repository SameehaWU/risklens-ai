The Problem

Banking compliance teams manage AML, fraud, and regulatory reporting with manual, siloed tools. The result: missed signals, slow investigations, and audit gaps.

Volume

Hundreds of thousands of transactions daily requiring monitoring against multiple overlapping regulations

Speed

SAR filing within 30 days, CTR within 15 days, continuing activity reviews every 90 days

Complexity

BSA, FATF, FinCEN, UAE AML/CFT — analysts must cross-reference activity against multiple regulatory frameworks

Auditability

Every decision must be traceable to specific evidence and policy. No single tool connects data to regulation to finding

Our Solution

An end-to-end AML copilot that combines transaction data with policy text, letting compliance users ask questions and get governed, evidence-backed answers — from signal to finding to report.
